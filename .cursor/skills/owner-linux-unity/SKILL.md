1. Close Unity.
2. Download https://github.com/GreatSaiyaDave/ProjectARGON/archive/refs/heads/main.zip

After they have the new copy, later updates: **WRLDZ → Get Latest from GitHub**. Proof: `WRLDZ_BUILD` = `CLEAN-0907`.
