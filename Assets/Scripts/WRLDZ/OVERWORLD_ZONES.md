# Overworld zones (canon)

Live **map pins**: Tear · Raid · Bazaar · Training · Story · PvP.  
**Tournaments are not pins** — they are **rooms**. One player hosts from the Eye; the match starts when enough duelists join (or the host fills seats).  
**Street NPCs are not pins** — they wander around Tear spawn points.  
**Tears** are this game's PokéStop: walk up, harvest (cooldown), fight wanderers, climb to the Tear boss.

| Zone | Job | Encounter |
|------|-----|-----------|
| **Tear** | Spirit rifts. PokéStop-style harvest (SE + set orb). Walking closer spawns more street duelists. Rural Tears spread NPCs farther. | Street 4000 / 8000 LP. Tear boss 10–15k LP after **3 straight 8000 LP wins** around that Tear. Tome legal on the boss. |
| **Raid** | Isolated boss. Built for **3-on-1**. Solo is allowed. Empty seats fill with AI allies until real 3-player networking ships. | 20,000+ LP. Tome legal. Engine is still 1v1 vs the boss; party size is recorded on the match. |
| **Bazaar** | Economy only. | Buy/sell, auctions, altar of fire (cards → Set Energy), stone tablets (SE → packs). |
| **Training** | Practice. | 0 XP. Mr. Referobot or other players. No stakes. |
| **Story** | Umbrax fractures in the YGO timeline. | Portal + mission preview + cutscene → era duel. **LOB / MRD / SRL** start unlocked. **Set orbs** drop from Tear harvests, street NPC wins, and PvP wins through **level 50**. |
| **PvP** | Ranked player duels. | PvP rewards. No Set Energy. Set orbs yes (through L50). |

## Street NPCs (Tear satellites)

- Spawn at Tear points. Wander inside that Tear's **rural radius**.
- **Ruralness:** count other Tears within 1.2 km. Fewer Tears → larger NPC radius so players in the middle of nowhere still find duelists.
- Count and 8000 LP mix **rise as the player walks closer**.
- Two bands: **4000 LP** and **8000 LP**.
- Must win **one 4000 LP street duel every 24 hours** to keep 8000 LP access.
- Closer to the Tear → harder mix.
- Tap NPC → placeholder **Possession** scene (human → Tear opens → spirit takes the body → aether disk) → AR / Zone Mode duel.

## Tournaments (rooms)

- Not a map pin. Open **TOURNEY** from the Eye anywhere.
- Host a 4- or 8-seat room. Others join. **START** when occupied ≥ min.
- Lab: **FILL AI** so a room can start without a live network.

## Raid 3-on-1

- Design lock: **three duelists vs one 20k+ LP boss**, Tome legal.
- Now: **SOLO** (you vs the boss) or **3-ON-1 AI** (two AI ally seats recorded; still one duel vs the boss).
- Later: three real players share the raid instance / HP pool.

## Set orbs

- Always unlocked: **LOB · MRD · SRL**.
- 8 orbs unlock the next ladder set (PSV → … → TLM) through **L50**.
- Sources: Tear harvest, street NPC win, PvP win. **Not** from Set Energy / tablet rules (PvP still grants **no SE**).
- After L50, remaining sets follow story / era cadence.

## Tome

+1 page every 10 duelist levels (max 10). Classic S/T (MST, Gift of the Mystical Elf, Just Desserts, …) playable **once per turn as if in hand** during **Tear boss** and **Raid** duels only.
